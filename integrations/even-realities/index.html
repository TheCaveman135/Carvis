Connect to Carvis
  1. In Carvis, enable Assistant engine and Even Realities under Integrations.
  2. Make your Carvis server reachable from your phone using your LAN, VPN, Tailscale, or a hosted address. Copy its HTTP or HTTPS address and generate a pairing token in Even Realities → Settings.
  3. Paste them here. Add Voice conversation and Hear Carvis speak for voice features.

Use the server address this companion was packaged for. Your Carvis password and AI API keys do not belong here.

Glasses and audio

Enable Assistant engine and Even Realities in Carvis. Voice conversation adds microphone input; Hear Carvis speak adds spoken replies.

The resting glasses screen is otherwise completely blank. Replies appear below your widgets.

Keep this screen active for iPhone playback. Home speaker playback works independently of the phone screen.

Tap to enable the British iPhone speech voice. Keep this screen active for phone playback.

Widget controls

Slots run down the left column (1, 2), then the right (3, 4). Swipe to select. Tap a button to act; tap a slider or dropdown, swipe to adjust, then tap to apply.

Double-tap removes the selection outline and cancels an unfinished adjustment. Widgets stay. Use Clear screen in the glasses app menu to remove them. With nothing selected, a single tap controls your microphone.

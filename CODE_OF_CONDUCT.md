# Code of Conduct

Carvis welcomes people who want to use, improve, or discuss the project.
Participate with respect and assume good intent while remaining open to
correction.

Expected behavior includes giving constructive feedback, explaining technical
disagreements, and respecting people's time and privacy. Harassment,
discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized attention, and sharing
someone else's private information are not acceptable in issues, pull
requests, reviews, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and restrict
participation to keep these spaces useful and safe. Decisions should consider
the behavior, its impact, and any repeated pattern.

To report abusive content or a conduct concern, use GitHub's **Report content**
option on the relevant issue, pull request, or comment. For concerns about
content you cannot report there, use [GitHub's abuse reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Do not post another person's private details in a public report.
